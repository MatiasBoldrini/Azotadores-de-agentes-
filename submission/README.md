# SELENARC submission materials

Supporting files for the NASA Space Apps 2026 Lunar Landing Site Navigator entry (team Azotadores de Agentes). The live app is at https://siteforge-moon.vercel.app. This folder does not change application code, data, or tests.

## Contents

- `submission.md` — written submission text
- `pitch_v3/` — 7-slide pitch (`index.html`, `pitch.pdf`, `render.py`, fonts and images)
- `video/selenarc-demo-16x9-final.mp4` — 29.46 s SELENARC demo film, plus contact sheet (`selenarc-demo-16x9-final-sheet.png`). Earlier cut kept as `video/siteforge-demo-16x9-final.mp4`.
- `video_brief.md` — brief used to produce the film
- `video-remotion/` — Remotion source for the film, including the `selenarc-16x9-final` composition
- `screens_v7/` — desktop and mobile screenshots of the build used in the deck and film
- `validation_pgda69.md` and `validation_pgda69_scatter.png` — comparison to NASA LOLA PGDA 69
- `validation_pgda69/` — scripts, compact JSON outputs, and PDS labels (raw rasters omitted; see that folder’s README)

## Not included here

- Competitor and judge analysis: not present as files under the working materials directory
- Earlier video cuts (`siteforge-demo-16x9.mp4`, `-v2.mp4`) and `.bak` drafts
- NASA PGDA 69 GeoTIFFs larger than 10 MB (listed in `validation_pgda69/README.md`)
