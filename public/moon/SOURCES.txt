Moon imagery and relief used by the SITE//FORGE landing globe (visual only).

Global color: lroc_wac_gray.jpg
  NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit, lroc_color_poles_4k.tif (LROC WAC, 2019), 4096x2048.
  Page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720 (Visualizer: Ernie Wright)
  Desaturated to neutral gray with modest contrast; format and grade only.

South polar cap: wac_south_summer.jpg
  LROC WAC South Pole Summer Mosaic, 32 ppd (WAC_ROI_SOUTH_SUMMER_032P).
  Credit: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University (LROC)
  https://pds.lroc.im-ldi.com/data/LRO-L-LROC-5-RDR-V1.0/LROLRC_2001/EXTRAS/BROWSE/WAC_ROI/WAC_ROI_SOUTH_SUMMER/WAC_ROI_SOUTH_SUMMER_032P.TIF
  Polar stereographic, lat_0=-90, lon_0=0, R=1737400 m, 947.60470075464 m/px,
  3038x3038, upper-left (-1439293.09, 1439293.09) m. Brightness matched to the
  global gray in the 55-62 S overlap.

Relief: ldem_4_displacement.png
  NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit, ldem_4_uint.tif (LOLA), 1440x720, 4 pixels/degree,
  unsigned 16-bit half-meters with a +10000 m offset. Retrieved from the Internet
  Archive snapshot of the official URL:
  https://web.archive.org/web/20261003175921id_/https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/ldem_4_uint.tif
  (2076866 bytes, SHA-256 e6668bec27fc9b8fbb02d198c7ddfb08eedeeb790167b494f95e6b34201da05e)
  Converted to 8-bit, linearly stretched to the file range -8878.5 m .. 10504.0 m.
  The globe shows this relief with the same 4x vertical exaggeration as the site view.

Loading placeholders: landing-placeholder.jpg, landing-placeholder-portrait.jpg
  Small renders of this globe (the textures above) at the landing's opening view,
  on landscape and on phone-shaped portrait screens, shown while those textures
  download on slow connections.

Close-up terrain: public/site01/ (see meta.json)
  NASA PGDA LOLA product 78, Site01 5 m DEM, the same file the engine uses:
  https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_5mpp/Site01/Site01_final_adj_5mpp_surf.tif
  (40980810 bytes, SHA-256 3ba7b97cb00a2bcf21189c3aeb535f65afc21207154ab9f0d43c5bdc1f7e747e)
  elevation.f32: the engine's 5 m window [1344, 1344, 512, 512], every sample.
  surround-10m.u16: mean of 2x2 source pixels over window [800, 800, 1600, 1600] (8 km),
    SHA-256 bfffc08a6adbe145e0eaa7072507c218612e075c7fe1440cbc90311773484479
  surround-20m.u16: mean of 4x4 source pixels over the full extent [0, 0, 3200, 3200] (16 km),
    SHA-256 c3cd6b3aa88f2843afa437dcd607e903a4ec41b4ebd65317dc0f710eb9b36df8
  Unsigned 16-bit, elevation = -1000 m + value x 0.05 m. Drawn with the same 4x exaggeration.

None of these files feed the science engine. Visibility numbers come only from
public/data/snapshot.json.
